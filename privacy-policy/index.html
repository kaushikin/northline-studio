Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 September 2026. Website: gonorthline.uk. Contact: hello@gonorthline.uk

1. Introduction

Welcome to ANPAVAAM, operated via Northline Studio (https://www.gonorthline.uk/). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal information you may provide while visiting our website or interacting with our content, ads, and social media channels.

2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information to improve user experience and deliver our digital content:

  • Log data and analytics: like most websites, we collect standard server log data, including IP addresses, browser types, referring pages, and time spent on our site for technical performance monitoring.
  • Cookies: we use cookies to analyze site traffic, personalize content, and optimize advertising performance through platforms like Google Ads.
  • Voluntary contact information: if you reach out to us via email, WhatsApp, or contact forms, we collect the details you provide (such as your name and email address) solely to respond to your inquiry.

We do not collect or store sensitive personal information through our Google Ads campaigns.

3. Third-Party Services and Links

Our website and ad campaigns link to third-party platforms, including:

  • Google Ads: used for advertising and audience building. Google uses cookies (including advertising cookies) to serve ads based on visits to our site and other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ad Settings.
  • Social media and messaging platforms: we link to third-party services such as Telegram, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, and WhatsApp. These platforms operate under their own independent privacy policies, and we do not store or manage personal data submitted directly to these platforms.

4. How We Use Your Information

Any information collected is used exclusively to:

  • Operate and maintain our website and content platform.
  • Analyze campaign performance and traffic to improve user engagement.
  • Respond to direct communications or business inquiries.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or transfer your personal information to outside third parties.

5. Data Protection

We implement industry-standard security measures to safeguard any data processed through our website against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

6. Your Choices

You can refuse non-essential cookies in your browser settings. You can opt out of Google personalized ads at adssettings.google.com. To request deletion of contact details you sent us, email hello@gonorthline.uk.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

  • Website: https://www.gonorthline.uk/
  • Email: hello@gonorthline.uk
