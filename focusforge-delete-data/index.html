Request data deletion

This page is for the FocusForge Android app by FocusForge Labs. Follow the steps below to delete your data.

Steps to delete your data

1. Delete data stored on your phone (most users only need this).

  • Open Android Settings → Apps → FocusForge → Uninstall.
  • Uninstalling immediately deletes session history, distracting-app lists, and screen-time totals stored on that device.

2. If you activated a college license, also request the seat be released.

  • Email hello@gonorthline.uk
  • Subject: FocusForge data deletion
  • Include: the college activation code (for example FF-ABC-2026-001) and that you want your device seat released.
  • We will release the seat and stop associating that anonymous device fingerprint with the license. We aim to complete this within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • On the device, after uninstall: apps you marked as distracting, focus session start/end times, daily screen-time totals, local license cache, and app settings.
  • On the license server, after your email request: the device’s seat (anonymous fingerprint linked to that campus code).

What we do not keep

  • We do not keep names, emails, contacts, photos, payment cards, messages, or a list of apps you use as an account database.
  • We never collect screen time or session history off the phone.
  • Google Play handles Premium purchases. Refunds and Play purchase records are controlled by Google, not by FocusForge Labs.

Retention

  • On-device data: deleted when you uninstall. There is no extra retention period.
  • Campus seat: kept only while the license is active on that device, then released on your request, when the license expires, or if the device is no longer active. We do not keep usage history on the server.
